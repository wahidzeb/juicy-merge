About Juicy Merge

Juicy Merge is a fruit-dropping puzzle. Drop fruit into the jar, and when two of the same fruit touch they squish together into a bigger one. Keep merging up the fruit ladder and try to make a Watermelon before the jar overflows.

It looks simple, but a tidy jar makes all the difference. Combos, power-ups and different modes keep it interesting, and your progress is saved on your device.

3 quick tips

  1. Never drop a big fruit into the middle of small ones. It buries them.
  2. If the next fruit matches something on the surface, think about where both will land before you drop.
  3. A fruit only ends the game if it stays above the line for three seconds, so a bouncing fruit is not a disaster.

How to play

1Drag or move left and right to aim, then release or click to drop the fruit into the jar.

2When two of the same fruit touch, they merge into the next fruit up. Merge quickly in a row for COMBOS.

3Keep the jar below the red line. If a fruit stays above it for 3 seconds, the game is over.

4Power-ups: Shake stirs the jar, Pop removes one fruit, Upgrade grows a fruit one size.
