How to play

1Drag or move left and right to aim, then release or click to drop the fruit into the jar.

2When two of the same fruit touch, they merge into the next fruit up. Merge quickly in a row for COMBOS.

3Keep the jar below the red line. If a fruit stays above it for 3 seconds, the game is over.

4Power-ups: Shake stirs the jar, Pop removes one fruit, Upgrade grows a fruit one size.
